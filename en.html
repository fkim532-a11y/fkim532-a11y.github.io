Who I am

A retirement passion, taken seriously

My name is François. Retired, living in the Hautes-Pyrénées, near Lourdes, France, I've chosen to spend part of my time building apps that matter to me — tarot, oracles, dream interpretation, self-knowledge. Every app here is designed, illustrated and developed by one person, at their own pace. No team, no investors — just the wish to do things well.

— François
